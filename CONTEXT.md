# Nindova

Nindova is a bounded nightly ritual that replaces an unbounded late-night loop with a satisfying sequence that deliberately closes itself.

## Language

**Session**:
One complete nightly arc whose required effort decreases until it ends itself.
_Avoid_: Round, level, run

**Wall**:
A user-chosen boundary that redirects selected unbounded apps to Nindova at a chosen nightly time while preserving one unguilted way through.
_Avoid_: App blocker, lockout, parental control

**Gate**:
The Wall surface that offers entry into Nindova or immediate dismissal.
_Avoid_: Warning, intervention, punishment

**Dismissal**:
The single “Not now” choice that removes the Wall for the rest of that night without guilt, justification, penalty, or repeated prompting; the Wall re-arms at the next scheduled night. Dismissal never blocks Nindova, which remains available for voluntary entry that night.
_Avoid_: Failure, skip, override

**Visitor**:
An animal or boat that completes the Vista passage and settles into the scene.
_Avoid_: Reward, collectible, unlock

**Echo**:
A quiet, non-numeric trace of a previously settled Visitor that is unaffected by nights when no Session occurs.
_Avoid_: Trophy, history count, attendance marker

**Night ID**:
The immutable Dawn date, captured IANA time zone, and recipe version that select one deterministic night. It is captured once when a Session begins and cannot change when midnight, daylight-saving time, or the device zone changes.
_Avoid_: Daily seed, streak day, attendance date

**Dawn**:
The before-noon return to the previous Session's Vista, rendered at first light as the product's only reward-bearing surface.
_Avoid_: Daily reward, check-in bonus, morning streak

**Dawn Keepsake**:
A shareable still image or subtle looping clip made from Dawn without adding scores, counts, or attendance signals.
_Avoid_: Achievement card, completion badge

**Drift**:
The final interactive act in which one warm light follows touch with increasing independence before continuing on its own.
_Avoid_: Free play, sandbox, endless mode

**Handoff**:
The graceful moment when Drift stops requiring touch and commits the Session to its ending.
_Avoid_: Timeout, cutoff, failure

**Required Action**:
An action needed to advance a Session; it must work with one finger, cannot fail, and has a semantic non-Canvas equivalent.
_Avoid_: Challenge, precision gesture, skill check
