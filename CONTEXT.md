# Nindova

Nindova is a bounded nightly game that replaces an unbounded late-night loop with a simple mental exercise and an ending it cannot bargain away.

## Language

**Session**

One complete nightly arc from voluntary entry through Rasoi Pairs to the quiet end card.
_Avoid_: Round, level, run

**Rasoi Pairs**

The fixed 36-tile pair-removal exercise inspired by Mahjong solitaire. It is not traditional four-player Mahjong.
_Avoid_: Mahjong clone, challenge mode

**Tile**

One kitchen form on the board. A Tile can be free, selected, covered, or settled.
_Avoid_: Prize, collectible, card

**Masala Mound**

The fixed three-layer Rasoi board. A Tile is free when no Tile overlaps it from above and at least one horizontal side is open.
_Avoid_: Level, rack, challenge board

**Safe pair**

Two matching free Tiles. Help may identify a Safe pair but cannot remove it.
_Avoid_: Winning move, hint currency

**Brass bloom**

The brief, uniform visual and optional sound response when a legal pair leaves the board. It is predictable confirmation, not a prize or dopamine claim.
_Avoid_: Kaboom, reward burst, combo

**Settle**

The uniform response when a pair leaves the board or when the hidden production boundary closes an unfinished Session.
_Avoid_: Score, clear bonus, failure

**Night ID**

The immutable Dawn date, captured IANA time zone, and recipe version that select one deterministic board. It is captured once when a Session begins.
_Avoid_: Daily seed, streak day, attendance date

**Dawn**

The before-noon return to the previous Session’s kitchen forms, rendered at first light as the product’s only between-Session pull.
_Avoid_: Daily reward, check-in bonus, morning streak

**Dawn keepsake**

A user-initiated local still or subtle silent loop made from Dawn without scores, counts, or attendance signals.
_Avoid_: Achievement card, completion badge

**Wall**

A deferred native boundary that may redirect selected unbounded apps to Nindova at a chosen nightly time. It is not implemented in this repository.
_Avoid_: Shipped feature, app blocker

**Dismissal**

The single “Not now” choice. In the future Wall it removes the Gate for the night without guilt; Nindova remains available for voluntary entry later that night. The browser intake demonstrates the same agency without claiming native interception.
_Avoid_: Failure, skip penalty, lockout
