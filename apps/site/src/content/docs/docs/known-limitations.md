---
title: Known limitations
description: Honest boundaries of the current browser-first foundation.
---

The initial public foundation preserves a concept demo; it is not the completed product.

- The portrait camera and semantic controls are browser-verified, but real-device VoiceOver and TalkBack acceptance is still pending.
- The current demo exposes reviewer pacing and replay controls that are not suitable for production.
- Night recipes and room memory are not yet deterministic or persisted.
- Dawn still and loop export are not yet implemented.
- The installable offline PWA and service-worker privacy gates are not yet implemented.
- Fifteen-minute production wall-clock verification is still pending.
- The iOS Wall is deferred. No entitlement, native interception, notification, or App Store capability is claimed.

These limits are Must-scope work in the approved build plan, not hidden follow-ups.
