Rasoi Pairs is inspired by the readable free-tile rule of Mahjong solitaire. It is not traditional four-player Mahjong and does not copy a Mahjong tile set.

## The path

1. **Enter or decline** — “Not now” opens a quiet dismissal surface. Nindova stays voluntarily available later that night.
2. **Read the mound** — six Tiles are initially free. A Tile needs no overlap from above and at least one open horizontal side.
3. **Choose a pair** — select two free tiles showing the same kitchen form. A mismatch simply lifts the newer tile; there is no penalty.
4. **Settle** — a match folds inward through one dim, predictable brass bloom and an optional restrained sound. Newly uncovered or open-side Tiles become free.
5. **Ask for help if wanted** — a safe pair breathes briefly. Help never removes tiles.
6. **Close** — the cleared board settles under its brass lid. If the hidden production boundary arrives first, the remaining kitchen settles under the same lid and reaches the same end card.

The end card begins: “The session is over. That's the point.” Its primary action opens an optional screen-away handoff. Neither surface routes back into play. Closing and deliberately reopening Nindova returns to intake, where another separately bounded same-night Session is possible.

## Board guarantee

The board has 36 Tiles: 24 on the base, 8 in the middle, and 4 on top, with four copies of each of nine motifs. The same overlap/open-side legality kernel drives rendering, input, help, active-session validation, and exhaustive verification.

The verifier explores all 382 reachable removal states for every tested board recipe. Every nonterminal state has at least one legal pair; every legal choice reaches the single terminal state. Nightly variation only permutes motif placement and cloth tone.

## Rest boundary

“Dim and rest” asks the person to put down the phone, close their eyes, picture one ordinary kitchen object, and let it go. This is an unvalidated product hypothesis, not CBT-I, a sleep treatment, or a proven method for sleep onset.

## Timing

The production clock begins an unfinished board's automatic settle at twelve minutes and has a hidden fifteen-minute ceiling. The remaining kitchen closes under the same lid. The reviewer switch `?review=1` compresses those boundaries without changing order or outcome.
