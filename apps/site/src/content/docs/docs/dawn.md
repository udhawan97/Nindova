title: Dawn

Dawn is the only reward-bearing surface in Nindova. It does not appear at night and no notification asks a person to return.

## Eligibility

Dawn is available from 06:00 through 11:59 on the completed Session’s captured Dawn date. Evaluation always uses the IANA time zone stored when that Session began, even if the device later travels or changes zones.

Before 06:00, from noon onward, after a skipped date, without a completion, or after corrupt-state recovery, no stale Dawn is shown.

## First light

The renderer uses the exact completed `nightId`, Vista, featured Visitor, Echoes, boats, and deterministic color details. The mustard meadow or riverside harbor returns at first light with a restrained phulkari edge. No score, sleep metric, attendance mark, or completion badge appears.

## Keepsakes

- **Save still** creates a local PNG from the rendered canvas.
- **Share still** uses the browser’s explicit file-sharing capability when available.
- **Make silent 3-second loop** records only the canvas video track in a supported MP4 or WebM format.

If loop recording is unsupported or fails, the still and Dawn remain usable. Cancelling share leaves Dawn open. Preview object URLs are revoked when replaced, closed, or unloaded; download URLs are revoked immediately after handoff. The exporters add no product metadata, telemetry, or remote request.
