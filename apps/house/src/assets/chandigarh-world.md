# Chandigarh world artwork

`chandigarh-world.webp` is original AI-generated environment art made for Nindova using the built-in imagegen tool, September 2026. No third-party photo or map was imported. The source output is 1536×1024; the shipped WebP uses quality 88. The asset is static terrain; characters, camera, water accents, power-ups, jumps, dashes, obstacles and pickups are drawn and controlled by the game.

## Final generation prompt

Create one production environment painting for an original top-down exploration game inspired by Chandigarh. Landscape 3:2, highest detail. A playable overhead miniature-city background, no UI, text, labels, borders or people. Orthographic three-quarter overhead view, rich tactile painted materials, warm late-afternoon sun, green trees, sandstone pavement, terracotta and turquoise water. Fill the frame with terrain; no sky or floating island. Lower left: low-rise flat-roof Sector 22 brick/plaster homes, gardens and a welcoming ochre front door. Lower right: Shastri-style cloth market arcades, shaded verandahs, fabric awnings and baskets. Center: broad Sector 17-style plaza with a modest fountain, repeated modernist commercial bays and a cinema silhouette. Upper left: Rose Garden beds and intersecting paths. Upper center: original Rock Garden-inspired stone/mosaic courtyards and waterfalls, no replicas of artworks. Upper right: Sukhna-inspired lake, docked boats and wide stone promenade. Broad connected cream paths, an east-west road around 70% height and north-south green boulevard. Parked bicycles, lamps, benches, planters, water channels, pots and shutters. Keep canopies off main paths; consistent perspective and scale. A fictional compressed neighborhood, never a real navigation map. No logos, collectibles, magical beams, HUD, panels or blur.

The renderer's placement mesh was authored against the inspected output. Verified setting references: [Chandigarh homecoming](../../../../docs/research/chandigarh-homecoming.md).

## Character atlas

`gurpreet-walk.webp` is a second original built-in imagegen output, 1254 square, optimized to WebP quality 90 with alpha preserved. `gurpreet-frames.json` records alpha bounds in its 4×4 cells. No visual paint-over was applied. Final prompt: create a true-transparent 4×4 atlas of the same adult Punjabi man, peacock-blue kurta, cream trousers, tan shoulder bag, dark shoes, short swept hair and moustache; detailed painterly miniature-game art viewed three-quarter overhead. Rows face south, east, west, north. Columns idle, two walking phases, jump. Consistent scale and floor anchor, each character fully within its cell, no labels, backgrounds, shadows, franchise assets or effects. The renderer chooses direction and movement frames; idle never cycles.
