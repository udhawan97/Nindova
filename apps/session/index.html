Nindova is a behavioral design study about ending an unbounded loop. It is not a sleep tracker or treatment, and Masala Mound has not been clinically shown to make people sleepy. Intended for adults 18 and over. Persistent sleep difficulty deserves evidence-based care such as CBT-I with a qualified clinician.
