# Keep the Wall user-chosen and unguilted

The Wall will suggest social, video, news, and browser apps without preselecting any; the user will type the nightly time directly; and the Gate will provide one quiet “Not now” action that removes the Wall until the next scheduled night. Nindova itself remains available for voluntary entry later that night. This preserves a meaningful precommitment without repeated nagging, punitive blocking, or delegating the user's intent to a system schedule.
