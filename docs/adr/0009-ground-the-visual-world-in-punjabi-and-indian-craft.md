# Ground the visual world in Punjabi and Indian craft

Nindova’s room and Vistas will use a distinctly Punjabi and Indian visual language. The primary cues are phulkari geometry, indigo and marigold, madder red, terracotta, aged brass, carved sheesham-toned wood, a mustard meadow, a quiet riverside harbor, and locally familiar small objects and Visitors.

The direction is material and environmental, not religious or nationalistic. It must not fall back to generic “exotic” ornament, festival imagery, flags, sacred symbols, or a decorative collage of unrelated Indian references. English remains the interface language for this build; localization is a separate product decision.

The fixed state path, accessibility equivalents, decay, closure, and behavioral claims do not change with the theme.
