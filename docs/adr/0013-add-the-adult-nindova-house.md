# Add the adult Nindova House while preserving the Night Room

Nindova now targets adults aged 18 and over. The public entry becomes **Nindova House**, an architectural hub with two deliberately separate product loops:

- The **Grand Salon** contains authored entertainment games. The initial tables are Pattern Court, Mirror Forge, Stack Architect, and Lantern Ledger. Each has five fixed chapters and a deterministic curtain call. A person may replay one only by choosing it again.
- The existing `/play/` experience becomes the **Night Room**. Its Two-Loop Law, hidden fifteen-minute maximum, two immutable core lines, local Night/Dawn schemas, closure behavior, and `window.__ct` evidence boundary remain protected and regression-tested. Adults-18+ audience and disclaimer framing is appended without changing those core lines.

The Salon may use visible chapter position, escalating authored challenge, replay, and deterministic action-specific celebration because those mechanics stay inside an explicitly chosen entertainment game. It still prohibits leaderboards, streaks, randomized rewards, attention-pressure language, social comparison, and telemetry. The Gallery stores at most one replaceable entertainment result per game. Every stored result must include `mode: "entertainment"`, a schema version, game version, ruleset version, run identifier, and narrow completion facts; it must not contain a population comparison or inferred cognitive trait.

Entertainment play is not a validated assessment. A future research build may use the same input and rendering primitives only after it has a versioned construct definition, representative adult norming sample, preregistered scoring plan, reliability and validity evidence, fairness analysis, uncertainty reporting, informed consent, independent ethics review, and explicit data governance. Until those gates exist, the production product must not emit or market an IQ estimate, intelligence result, percentile, diagnosis, or equivalent proxy. No data collection is authorized by this decision.

The adult acknowledgement is audience framing, not identity or date-of-birth verification. It stores only a local acknowledgement. This decision supersedes the global one-game-menu and 13+ portions of the earlier contract while preserving them inside the Night Room where applicable.
