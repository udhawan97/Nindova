# Deepen Sector Sprint flight and illustration

Sector Sprint keeps its fixed five-Act, self-closing entertainment route and deepens the moment-to-moment play as an original thrust-and-glide action comedy. A single pointer held anywhere on the stage or on the Lift control raises the lead; release settles the lead into a glide. The Act tool stays a separate large control. Keyboard Lift/Glide, Dash/Vault/Stomp, and Act-tool expression remains available on Mac without becoming necessary for completion. Pointer cancellation, orientation changes, blur, pause, and route exit all release held thrust. The complete narrated route remains the non-gesture completion path.

The engine advances at a fixed 60 Hz simulation step behind a foreground-active accumulator. A frame can catch up through at most the same two-second active-time cap consumed by the House session clock; longer background or main-thread gaps cannot charge unseen progress. An authored ceiling keeps a continuous hold within the visible flight lane and absorbs upward velocity rather than banking a hidden launch. The five Acts still last exactly 32 active seconds each and retain the 240-second whole-table boundary. Input changes choreography only: automatic forward travel, Act order, access, closure, Gallery provenance, and timing never depend on dexterity.

Each Act gains one broad, deterministic, full-height comic-complication gate. Sabzi Load briefly softens vertical travel; Monsoon Headwind changes scarf trails, particles, and scenery treatment only, so the drawn lead never separates from the authoritative collision body. Every complication has authored arrival and closing copy, one bounded duration, no random selection, no count, rarity, inventory, missed-item message, stored progression, or access effect. A fresh Act and a reloaded House contain no complication state.

The lead presentation may use the project-owned transparent sprite sheet documented in `apps/house/src/assets/sector-sprint-characters.md`, with the existing code-drawn figure as a non-blocking fallback. The sheet contains original Gurpreet and Harjit poses and no borrowed game character, logo, level, or asset. Canvas 2D remains the renderer. No Pixi or other runtime is added: a library comparison is justified only if measured Canvas work later misses a defined authoring, size, memory, or frame-pacing target.

The runtime art budget is one compressed sheet no larger than 1.5 MB and approximately 6 MiB decoded RGBA memory. Device pixel ratio remains capped at 2. After 90 representative foreground frames, the renderer selects `high` at p95 frame intervals up to 21 ms, `balanced` up to 38 ms, and `quiet` above 38 ms. Lower tiers remove only ambient particles, shake, and decorative motion; route state, collision geometry, input, timing, copy, and semantic status are invariant. These thresholds are engineering safeguards, not proof of readiness on a physical phone. Current physical-device sustained four-minute performance, thermal behavior, and native touch latency remain unverified until tested on the declared device floor.

The portrait presentation enlarges and crops the same logical `960 × 432` stage around the lead rather than changing simulation coordinates. The generated character is always drawn at the same fixed logical player anchor used by collision projection. Reduced motion suppresses ambient camera and particle treatment, and narrated play remains the full alternate presentation.

Audio stays locally synthesized, optional, capped, and non-blocking. Pause, hide, blur, or an exit confirmation suspend active voices and the audio context. A valid later interaction may resume it. The context closes only when sound is disabled, the game instance is destroyed, narration replaces action, the route finishes, or the route exits. No network request, telemetry, speech playback, or required audio cue is introduced.

This decision does not add scores, high scores, lives, health, failure, endless play, levels, randomized rewards, assessment, intelligence estimates, sleep grades, fatigue claims, or persistence beyond the existing replaceable entertainment completion fact. It does not modify Rasoi Pairs, Dawn, or the Night Room Two-Loop Law.
