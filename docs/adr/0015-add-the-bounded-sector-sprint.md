# Add the bounded Sector Sprint table

Sector Sprint is the fifth Grand Salon table: an original, code-drawn side-scrolling Chandigarh comedy for adults, led by Gurpreet and his mother Harjit. It keeps the requested automatic forward motion, jumps, harmless message sparks, escalating authored scenes, Punjabi-family wit, and five-Act city journey while rejecting the requested endless route, score, high score, failure loop, and fatigue-to-sleep framing.

The action route has five fixed Acts: Ghar Wapsi, Sabzi Command, Baraat Detour, Monsoon Protocol, and Roti Relay. Each Act advances for exactly 32 seconds of foreground-active engine time, then moves through its own deterministic House praise. The engine and whole-table clock consume the same bounded frame delta, so a long main-thread stall cannot charge unseen Act time. Optional object density rises from five in the opening lane to nine in the home lane; the later Acts add original mandi, baraat, monsoon, and ghar scenery without introducing failure. The whole table also has a 240-second foreground-active boundary. Reaching that boundary before all five Acts closes the table without storing or displaying a completion. Pausing, hiding, or blurring suspends Acts and inter-Act transitions and resets the animation timestamp so background time cannot be charged on return.

Jump and a context-specific message spark are optional. Sparks can transform only allowlisted inanimate abstractions such as missed-call bubbles, price tags, puddles, produce baskets, route bubbles, streamers, and grocery lists. The game has no human, animal, vehicle, or building target. Collisions produce a visible witty line and never remove progress, reset an Act, create health, or create failure.

The presentation is a token-driven moving miniature theatre rather than an imported arcade skin. Each Act has a deterministic Chandigarh skyline and its own authored street layer: late-return lamps, mandi stalls, baraat ribbons, monsoon rain, or the home approach. Gurpreet and Harjit are larger, role-specific code-drawn figures with bounded run, jump, landing, collision, and transformation responses. Camera movement is capped at two logical pixels, spark effects are capped at 24 particles, active spark objects are capped at four, and no effect uses randomness or carries semantic progress. Reduced motion freezes parallax and suppresses camera, impact, landing, and flourish effects.

Canvas drawing keeps a fixed `960 × 432` logical stage and scales its backing surface up to a device-pixel-ratio cap of 2. Resize redraws the current state without advancing the engine. Optional action and curtain cues share one synthesized audio context; sound starts off, creates no queued resume cue, and is closed when sound is disabled or the route pauses, hides, blurs, exits, narrates, finishes, or reaches its boundary. Audio failure remains non-blocking.

The complete narrated route contains three authored city beats for every Act and reaches the same curtain call, celebration, and `mode: "entertainment"` provenance. Reduced-motion visitors begin on that route. No timed response, precision movement, audio, animation, or visual interpretation is required to complete the table: Pause holds the authored boundary, remains paused between narrated Acts, and leaves the narrated beat controls available. A semantic Pause or Resume control remains present in either route.

Active session storage contains only `gameId`, authored Act position, local run identifier, and narrated story-beat position. Coordinates, elapsed time, input history, collisions, transformed objects, and performance summaries remain in memory and are not persisted. Because the remaining boundary budget is deliberately not stored, a Sector Sprint document reload fails closed to the Grand Salon, clears the active marker, explains the exit, and records no completion. Completion keeps the existing replaceable five-chapter entertainment fact in the Gallery. There is no assessment input or output.

All art is drawn from project-owned TypeScript and existing House tokens. No borrowed game name, character, level, image, sound, package, or runtime service is shipped. Nindova remains free and open source under Apache-2.0, with optional sound synthesized locally and zero telemetry or third-party runtime request. The repository can verify the richer original presentation, fixed behavior, and technical boundaries; it cannot verify an “AAA” quality label, cultural authenticity, or player enjoyment without representative adult playtesting.

This decision does not change Masala Mound or any Night Room rule. It does not claim that Sector Sprint makes a person sleepy, tired, culturally represented, or cognitively measured; those outcomes have no supporting human evidence in this repository.
