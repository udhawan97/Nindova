# Rasoi Pairs redesign plan

**Status:** Implemented and verified for v0.1.0.

## Product promise

Nindova offers one simple mental exercise with a familiar rule: find two matching kitchen tiles that are free at the ends of their racks. The interaction is calm and tactile, the ending is guaranteed, and nothing turns the Session into a performance measure.

## Fixed experience

1. **Enter:** begin voluntarily or choose “Not now.” Choosing “Not now” never prevents a voluntary return later that night.
2. **Pair:** remove matching free tiles from a 36-tile Rasoi board. Three safe pairs are available at the start; newly exposed tiles replace them.
3. **Settle:** clearing the board ends the Session. If the production boundary arrives first, the remaining kitchen settles under its lid and reaches the same quiet ending.
4. **Dawn:** from 06:00 through 11:59 in the captured Night ID zone, the previous night's motifs return as a first-light kitchen composition that can be saved or shared locally.

## Non-negotiable checks

- One legality kernel serves rendering, input, help, and exhaustive board verification.
- Every reachable nonterminal state exposes at least one legal pair; every legal choice remains solvable.
- Same-night voluntary replay uses the identical board and cannot multiply Dawn or return state.
- No score, timer, streak, grade, achievement, collection, missed-night language, or randomized reward.
- Keyboard, switch, screen-reader, 200% zoom, reduced motion, muted audio, and denied audio all preserve completion.
- The long-lived local schema stores only the facts needed for Night ID, Dawn, and the quiet tomorrow intention. Interaction timing remains ephemeral.
- Runtime requests remain same-origin and static, with zero telemetry.

## Cultural direction

The motifs are everyday kitchen forms: belan, chakla, tawa, chimta, steel katori, tiffin, masala dabba, chai glass, and pressure cooker. Indigo, madder, marigold, brass, sheesham-toned wood, and restrained phulkari-inspired geometry carry the Punjabi material direction. Sacred symbols, flags, festival collage, and generic “exotic” ornament are excluded.

This is a Punjabi-inspired art direction, not a claim of completed human cultural-authenticity review. A Punjabi cultural reviewer remains a precondition for any stronger authenticity claim.

## Evidence boundary

Nindova is a behavioral design study about replacing an unbounded loop with a bounded one. It is not a sleep treatment, sleep tracker, or proven way to fall asleep. The pair exercise must not be described as clinically validated, dopamine-producing, or endorsed by CBT-I research.
