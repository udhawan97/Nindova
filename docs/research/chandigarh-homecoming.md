# Chandigarh homecoming — setting evidence and story opportunities

> Targeted primary-source research for the Sector Sprint redesign, 2026-09-10. This is a setting brief for an original fictional game, not a travel guide or a claim that the game accurately reproduces Chandigarh. The proposed interactions below are design hypotheses; they have not been validated with local players.

## Setting direction

Give the player a neighborhood to inhabit: recognizable materials, people to meet, and useful things to bring home. Chandigarh's identity can come from its sector structure, open public spaces, gardens, and everyday market architecture as well as famous landmarks. Sector 17 was conceived as a commercial center within a city of self-contained sectors; its plaza remains an open-air mix of shops, eateries, and gathering space. [Ministry of Tourism: Sector 17](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/chandigarh/chandigarh/sector-17)

Use an explicitly compressed, fictional geography. The official tourist map places the Rose Garden in Sector 16, the shopping plaza in Sector 17, and the Rock Garden and Sukhna Lake toward the northern part of the city. A small playable map can connect those places through stylized transitions, but must not imply that real journeys take seconds or that the route is suitable for navigation. [Chandigarh Tourism map](https://chandigarhtourism.gov.in/uploads/map.PDF)

## Five finite story opportunities

Every person, errand, dialogue, and item below is fictional. The real places supply context only. Choose a small coherent set rather than turning every landmark into an obligatory fetch quest.

1. **The market bag, Sector 22.** A fictional neighbor has left vegetables ready at a stall. Speak to the vendor and pack the bag; a nearby tailor ties its loose handle. The bag visibly changes, giving the departure a purpose. The errands and vegetable stall are invented, not a claim about Shastri Market's exact shops.
2. **The meeting place, Sector 17.** A friend's note says to meet near the fountain. Explore the plaza, recognize the landmark, and receive a family envelope. A choice of conversation gives the friend a personality; no precision test or race determines success.
3. **A moment in the roses, Sector 16.** Help a fictional gardener return a fallen label, then choose which rose to sketch for someone at home. Keep the drawing as a temporary story item; do not pick the flowers or build a persistent collectible album.
4. **Something made useful again, Rock Garden.** At an explicitly fictional supervised craft table, arrange three supplied colored pieces into an original tiny mosaic. The materials come from the table, never the monument. Offer a direct “Arrange with the artisan” action yielding the same result.
5. **The borrowed book, Sukhna.** Meet a relative on the lakeside path, take back a borrowed book, and sit for one short exchange before choosing the route home. Boat and water motion enrich the scene; sound and timing are unnecessary. The final home arrival acknowledges what the player actually did, then closes without a score or replay prompt.

## Production implications and evidence limits

- These sources support **recognizable setting cues**, not a promise that Chandigarh residents will enjoy the game. Local-player feedback remains the appropriate test of recognition, tone, and fun.
- Keep the core movement deliberate: the player remains still without input; tap destinations, directional controls, and an equivalent text-led route can all reach the same encounters. Show walking only while moving, and use optional ambient animation for the world.
- Favor cached scenery and a small bounded set of animated people, water highlights, foliage, and interaction responses. Richness can come from composition and material detail; this is a design approach, not measured performance evidence.
- Use the small task bag only inside this journey. Do not add random rewards, persistent collections, achievement counters, urgency, or fear of missing out. Going home early should remain a valid ending.
- Sources disagree about Rose Garden acreage and plant counts; omit those figures. Some official pages retain dated transport and opening-time information. This brief deliberately makes no current pricing, schedule, travel-time, or navigation claims.
- Pages and galleries are reference links, not asset licenses. No third-party photos, maps, logos, sculpture images, or audio were copied into the repository for this brief. Produce original art and keep generated or commissioned asset provenance with the implementation. Several official direct-page and image fetches timed out; search-indexed primary-page text supplied the remaining cited setting facts. No claim of visual inspection of those unavailable images is made.
