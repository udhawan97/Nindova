# Nindova v0.4.1 — House Continuity

Nindova v0.4.1 keeps movement through the five-door House predictable, makes local Gallery outcomes truthful under storage failure, and gives small-screen visitors an immediate next step. The eight games and the separate bounded Night Room keep their existing rules and outcomes.

## Highlights

- Browser Back and the visible House Back controls restore the prior list position; opening a door or table begins at the new view's top.
- A completion appears in the Gallery only after the canonical local write succeeds. If it fails, the curtain says so and retries the exact same run and completion time.
- Clearing the Gallery requires a count-scoped confirmation. Cancellation preserves every reading, and storage failure preserves a canonical snapshot across reload.
- The 320px House hero exposes the five doors immediately. A discarded Sector Sprint reload explains the safe closure and offers the same direct browse action.
- The adult-audience boundary keeps both choices visible at 320×568 and returns keyboard focus to the heading of the actual Home, door, Gallery, or table view.
- The House cache advances for the corrected PWA shell, and the audited transitive `nanoid` dependency is updated without changing runtime behavior.

## Evidence boundary

The release verifies 93 unit tests, the complete Chromium House suite, all eight completion flows, direct-link focus, browser and in-page Back restoration, compounded Gallery storage failure and reload, phone/desktop layouts, keyboard/nonvisual play, reduced motion, same-origin operation, and cold-offline House entry. Separate Playwright WebKit checks cover 320×568 and 1280×800 House surfaces, including the boundary, mobile hierarchy, Sector reload recovery, failed-write retry, and Gallery confirmation. Automation does not establish human enjoyment, real-device assistive-technology acceptance, installed Safari/Android PWA behavior, representative cultural review, or physical-device QR scanning.
