# Nindova v0.6.1 — The Long Way Home

Fixes the camera in short, wide browser windows. The viewport now fits within the painted world, removing the empty strip visible at 1280×720. Jumping, dashing, power-ups, courses and homecoming are unchanged from v0.6.0.

Verification includes camera-bound unit checks, a rendered right-edge regression, the complete action/narrated journey, phone layouts, and repeated active-play performance samples. The v0.6.0 release notes retain the broader redesign, research and testing details. Across three active-play samples per viewport at 4× CPU throttling, median sampled p95 frame intervals were 34.3 ms (1440×900), 33.2 ms (1280×720), and 17.5 ms on both phones. The worst desktop sample was 49.4 ms; all samples are retained, and these measurements are not a universal frame-rate guarantee. Native Safari and physical-device acceptance remain unverified.
