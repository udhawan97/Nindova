# Nindova v0.4.0 — Five Doors

Nindova v0.4.0 opens five category doors onto eight finite Grand Salon games while keeping the Night Room separate, bounded, and unchanged in purpose.

## Highlights

- **Pattern & Line** — Pattern Court and a placement-only Navakankari study.
- **Turn & Trap** — Mirror Forge and an Aadu Puli Aattam movement study.
- **Count & Carry** — Stack Architect and one-turn Pallanguzhi sowing studies.
- **Memory & Sequence** — Lantern Ledger.
- **Motion & Route** — Sector Sprint.
- Each classic addition is labeled an authored tactical rule study and shows its named source, documented scope, included mechanics, and omitted full-match rules.
- Existing tables gain richer sandstone, smoked-mirror, brass, rosewood, and velvet material treatment without changing their mechanics.
- `#door/<category>` and `#game/<game>` routes make the House directly addressable while preserving leave confirmation and return focus.
- The Gallery moves to schema 2 with a non-destructive, copy-on-write v1 fallback. The legacy key is never deleted automatically.

## Evidence boundary

The release verifies 91 unit tests, all eight House completion flows, phone/desktop layouts, 44px controls, keyboard/nonvisual play, reduced motion, local state migration, same-origin operation, cache migration, cold-offline House entry, Sector Sprint frame/input budgets, and the complete Night/Dawn regression suite. Automation does not establish a definitive cultural ruleset, ethnographic completeness, human enjoyment, real-device assistive-technology acceptance, or representative cultural review.

## Files

- `nindova-v0.4.0.html` — portable standalone Night Room; no service worker.
- `nindova-web-v0.4.0.zip` — complete static landing page, docs, House PWA, Night PWA, and standalone Night file.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` — SHA-256 digests for both release artifacts.
