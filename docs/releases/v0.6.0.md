# Nindova v0.6.0 — The Long Way Home

Sector Sprint becomes an original Chandigarh playground. Walk, jump and dash through a connected miniature city, try three traversal power-ups, finish the neighborhood errands, then walk home with Harjit.

## Highlights

- Explore a painted city inspired by Sector 17, Sector 22, the Rose Garden, Rock Garden and Sukhna Lake. The map and neighbors are fictional; the setting research separates official facts from creative interpretation.
- Spring juttis raise your jump, a monsoon scarf extends your dash, and a paper-wing pin lets you float longer. Spring pads, crates, moving rollers and airborne ribbons give the market, garden and courtyard their own short courses.
- Movement stops when you release the controls. Keyboard, touch and tap-to-walk are supported; optional guided help and a complete narrated route provide the same errand objects and homecoming without precision, motion or sound.
- The README's canonical Nindova mark now appears throughout the House, Night Room, documentation, icons and portable HTML. Category entrances and layouts have received a further polish.

## Boundaries and verification

The journey closes after ten foreground-active minutes. Pause, hidden tabs, window blur and exit confirmation suspend it. Early homecoming and reload do not claim a completed journey. Power-ups, course progress, bag contents and position remain in memory. Existing Gallery results remain readable with their original game version. The separate Night Room retains its bounded ending and stable evidence interface.

Release verification covers unit checks, all eight House games, a complete action-course playthrough, narrated homecoming, keyboard cancellation, phone layouts, standalone Night, prefixed hosting, and first-offline entry with both new art assets decoded. The original map and directional character atlas are local WebP files with no new runtime dependency or external requests.

Local Chromium verification used three active 120-frame samples per viewport with four-times CPU throttling, retaining every interval. Median sampled p95 was 34.2 ms on desktop in the adaptive balanced tier, and 17.5 ms in both phone viewports. Worst sampled p95 in the final run was 34.2 ms on desktop, 17.5 ms at 375px, and 17.6 ms at 320px; all nine final samples were below 35 ms. Earlier single-run desktop observations on this shared host reached 50.5 ms. These measurements do not establish universal frame rates or physical-device performance. Native Safari acceptance could not be completed because the local capture tool failed. Physical-device touch, thermal and assistive-technology acceptance remain unverified.

- `nindova-web-v0.6.0.zip` — complete root-hosted static website, docs, House PWA, Night PWA and standalone Night file. Unzip and serve over HTTP; PWA installation requires a secure origin.
